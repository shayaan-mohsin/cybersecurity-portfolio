# Scope and contributions

[Portfolio home](README.md)

This is Shayaan Mohsin’s learning and analysis portfolio. It presents public-data analysis, research, an offline cloud simulation, and academic or proposed designs.

The project work includes selecting questions, interpreting source material, designing investigation steps, and assembling the portfolio. AI assistance was used to draft and revise documentation, code, and visuals, including the audit-driven improvements in this revision. Reproducible files and tests let a reviewer inspect the resulting work. They do not establish independent authorship of every line.

## How to read the evidence

- **Public-data analysis:** input files and generated outputs are available. Results describe those files, not an entire industry or an employer’s environment.
- **Research:** source-supported claims are separated from proposed defensive work.
- **Offline simulation:** the cloud records are synthetic. Running the analyzer demonstrates local parsing and interpretation.
- **Design:** capstone and service desk material describes intended systems or scripted decisions. Runtime results remain to be collected.

The Serenity Bank capstone was an individual academic project. I authored the submitted report, “7.1 Putting It All Together.” This portfolio presents a revised summary of that design, with clarified diagrams and validation requirements. It does not claim a deployed banking system.

Historical planning files live in [the archive](archive/README.md). Their targets and progress labels are not evidence that a system was built.
