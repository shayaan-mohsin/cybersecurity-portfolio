# Threat profile and scope

[Project overview](README.md) · [Sources](sources-and-methodology.md)

Public reporting associated with Scattered Spider describes social engineering, abuse of identity recovery, account changes, and access to organizational information. The research question is how defenders could connect those actions across support and identity records.

The most useful trust boundary for this project is the moment a support interaction authorizes a security-sensitive account change. Good sign-in controls do not resolve a weak recovery process automatically.

The brief does not claim that every associated actor, campaign, or incident uses all selected techniques. It also does not attribute suspicious events solely from a matching behavior. Ordinary recovery, onboarding, travel, and approved administrative work can create similar records.
