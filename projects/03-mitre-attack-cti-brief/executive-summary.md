# Executive summary

[Project overview](README.md) · [Defensive walkthrough](detection-and-response.md)

This brief examines identity recovery as a security decision. Public threat reporting shows why support interactions, factor changes, and subsequent access should be reviewed together.

The selected ATT&CK v17.0 layer contains 11 source-supported techniques. It organizes research; it does not measure defensive coverage or demonstrate an intrusion.

The proposed next step is a controlled identity-recovery exercise that verifies authorization, logging, investigation, and restoration. Until that work is performed, the deliverable is a sourced design and a test plan.
