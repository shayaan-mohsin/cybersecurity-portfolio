The current claim is: “Designed service desk scenarios covering triage, access approvals, troubleshooting, user communication, and escalation.”

After execution, describe exactly which platform workflows and endpoint tests were performed, by which roles, on which release, with expected and actual results. A ServiceNow ticket record alone does not establish an account unlock or device repair.

Each genuine artifact should record scenario ID, actual platform record number, path, execution type, actor role, capture time and timezone, description, SHA-256, privacy review, and limitations. Keep raw private captures separate from reviewed public copies. A mockup belongs with design material, not operational evidence.
