# LinkedIn help-desk role research

Reviewed September 30, 2026. Four US postings were selected for accessible descriptions and relevant frontline support duties. This is a small, nonrandom sample, not a measurement of the job market or ticket frequency. Searches included both general help-desk terms and ServiceNow terms, so vendor representation is biased. Direct links lead to the job descriptions; descriptions below are paraphrased. No applications were submitted.

ServiceNow is the named working platform in 2/4 descriptions and one acceptable example in another. These observations support choosing ServiceNow for a learning project; they do not establish it as the most popular platform. Actual ticket prevalence requires an organization's operational data, which we do not have.

## How this becomes a portfolio

The eight core scenarios cover the recurring support duties; two extensions add outage coordination and MFA recovery. The [scenario catalog](../docs/scenario-catalog.md) maps each one to the responsibility it demonstrates. The customer messages and troubleshooting records are as important as the ticket fields.

For IT applications, lead with this project once real lab evidence exists. For cybersecurity applications, present it as an operations foundation beneath the analyst projects, with emphasis on identity checks, authorized access, and security escalation. It does not replace the proposed GRC, SOC, vulnerability, or identity flagships.

A polished draft demonstrates planning and writing. Credible ServiceNow experience requires actual records, agent-role execution, test results, and an honest account of limitations.
