# Executive brief: use the reports to guide questions

[Project overview](README.md) · [Detailed analysis](breach-trend-analysis.md)

**Decision supported:** where to begin a hypothetical healthcare control review.

The 100 retained reports contain 88 Hacking/IT Incident labels and a combined reported affected count of 6,692,288. One report accounts for 46.6% of that sum. The sample is a convenience sample and should not estimate sector-wide prevalence.

Server and email mentions make identity, sensitive-data access, monitoring, and recovery reasonable areas to investigate. The public fields do not show why each breach happened or whether a safeguard was absent.

I would first ask owners for inventories, access-review records, log coverage, and restore-test evidence. Confirmed gaps would enter the risk register with owners and validation criteria. No compliance finding or control-performance claim follows from these counts alone.
