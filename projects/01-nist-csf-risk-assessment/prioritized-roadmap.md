# Proposed roadmap

[Project overview](README.md) · [Risk register](risk-register.md)

This sequence is a planning exercise. Timing depends on the environment, staffing, and findings.

| Stage | Work | Evidence required before moving on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish scope | Agree on critical services, data owners, and reporting boundaries | An approved scope and current inventory |
| Validate hypotheses | Review access, recovery, logging, suppliers, and handling | Findings with affected systems and owners |
| Address confirmed gaps | Select changes based on business impact and feasibility | Approved changes, test plans, and rollback criteria |
| Verify results | Test changed controls and service restoration | Observable results, exceptions, and owner acceptance |
| Revisit risk | Review unresolved issues and changing dependencies | An updated register and explicit acceptance decisions |

No completion dates, risk reductions, or cost savings have been measured.
